# Truce: questions buyers and sellers ask

## Why was my refund denied?
AgentSafe gives a reason with every denial. The usual ones: no verified evidence yet (send the live photo with the challenge code next to the item), the amount is more than is held for the order, the order is not in a dispute, you have already received a refund in the last 30 days, or the proposal came from someone who is not the buyer of the order. The assistant cannot override a denial; it can offer the options from the compensation ladder.

## Why did my refund need a supervisor?
Refunds above the automatic limit (both an absolute cap and a percentage of the order) are escalated. A supervisor receives an email with the amount, your reason and the evidence verdict, and approves or rejects it with a signed link. You are told in the chat when that happens and when it is resolved.

## I sent a screenshot of my payment. Why is the order still unpaid?
Only the payment provider's signed confirmation marks an order as paid. Screenshots can be edited, so they are not accepted as proof. Use the hosted payment link.

## What is the challenge code for?
A fresh code that only you receive, written on paper next to the item in a new photo, proves the photo was taken now and of this item. An AI-generated or old photo cannot contain it. The assistant reports what it sees; the policy service decides what follows.

## I have the item and it is fine. How does the seller get paid?
Give the assistant the one-time delivery code you received by SMS. A matching code releases the held payout to the seller. Do not share the code before the item is in your hands.

## Can I get the full amount back?
Within the automatic limits a partial refund, store credit or replacement can be settled in the chat. A full refund above the limit is escalated to a supervisor; it is paid only if they approve.

## How long do I have?
Delivery codes are valid for 72 hours. Challenge codes for live photos are valid for 15 minutes. Supervisor approval links are valid for 48 hours.

## Who sees my evidence?
Both buyer and seller receive an evidence pack by email when a Truce is resolved: every policy decision, allowed and denied, the ledger entries, and the evidence verdicts.

## What can the seller do during a Truce?
Nothing is required. The payout stays held while evidence is collected; the seller is notified when it is resolved.
