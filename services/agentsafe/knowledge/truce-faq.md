# Truce: questions buyers and sellers ask

## Why was my refund denied?
AgentSafe gives a reason with every denial, and the assistant passes it on in plain words. The usual ones: no verified evidence yet (send the live photo or short video with the challenge code next to the item), the amount is more than is held for the order, the order is not in a dispute, the account already had a refund in the last 30 days, or the request came from someone who is not the buyer of the order. The assistant cannot override a denial; it can offer the standard Truce options.

## Why did my request need approval?
Requests above the automatic limit (both an absolute cap and a percentage of the order) need a human. So do requests that are within the limit but show policy drift: several denied requests on the same order, evidence that is inconclusive (low confidence, no damage visible, or an item that doesn't match the courier's pickup photo), a policy change since the payment was held, or an amount that isn't one of the standard options. The seller is asked first by email; if they reject it or don't answer in time, a Clasp supervisor decides. You are told in the chat when the decision is made.

## I sent a screenshot of my payment. Why is the order still unpaid?
Only the payment provider's signed confirmation marks an order as paid. Screenshots can be edited, so they are not accepted as proof. Use the hosted payment link.

## What is the challenge code for?
A fresh code that only you receive, written on paper next to the item in a new photo or short video, proves the evidence was captured now and shows this item. An AI-generated or old photo cannot contain it. The assistant reports what it sees; the policy service decides what follows.

## I have the item and it is fine. How does the seller get paid?
Give the assistant the one-time delivery code you received by text message. A matching code releases the held payout to the seller. Do not share the code before the item is in your hands. If the code never arrived, ask the assistant for a new one; it arrives in the WhatsApp chat and older codes stop working.

## Does the seller have to do anything?
No chat is needed. A courier collects the parcel and its signed pickup scan records dispatch. The seller is told when payment is secured, when a Truce opens, and when a decision needs their approval.

## Can I get the full amount back?
Within the automatic limits a partial refund, store credit or replacement can be settled in the chat. A full refund above the limit needs approval: the seller first, then a Clasp supervisor. It is paid only if one of them approves.

## What happens to the rest of the payment after a partial refund?
It goes to the seller automatically, as its own policy decision, so both sides end up whole. After a replacement, the seller's payout is released when you confirm the replacement's delivery.

## How long do I have?
Delivery codes are valid for 72 hours. Challenge codes for live photos and videos are valid for 15 minutes. The seller has 24 hours to answer an approval request and a supervisor has 48 hours.

## Who sees my evidence?
Both buyer and seller receive an evidence pack by email when a Truce is settled (ask the assistant to add your email address if you haven't): every policy decision, allowed, denied and escalated, the ledger entries, and the evidence verdicts.
